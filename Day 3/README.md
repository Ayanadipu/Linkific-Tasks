# Day 3 – Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## Task
Prepare a Business Requirements Document for a hypothetical Internal HR Leave Management System.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand the purpose and structure of a BRD.
- Learn the difference between business, functional, and non-functional requirements.
- Practice writing clear, specific, measurable, and testable requirements.
- Understand assumptions, constraints, stakeholders, and project scope.

## Deliverables
- Full BRD for the Internal HR Leave Management System.
- At least 8 business requirements.
- Functional and non-functional requirements.
- Assumptions and constraints.
- Short note explaining how a poorly written BRD can contribute to project failure.

## Project
Internal HR Leave Management System
