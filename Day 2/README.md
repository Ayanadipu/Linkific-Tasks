# Day 2 – Stakeholder Management and Communication Plan

## Task
Stakeholder Management and Communication Planning for a Hypothetical Mobile App Launch.

## Learning Objectives
- Understand how to identify project stakeholders.
- Learn the Power–Interest Matrix.
- Understand stakeholder communication planning.
- Identify internal and external stakeholders based on their influence and interest.

## Deliverables
- Stakeholder register with 6 stakeholders.
- Communication plan for the hypothetical project.
- Short note explaining the impact of poor stakeholder communication.
- Day 2 document prepared in Google Docs.

## Project
Hypothetical Mobile App Launch
